---
title: Pan the tactical view with two-finger scrolling
category: feature
release: 0.3.0
targets:
- type: system
  id: view-controls
  effect: added
credit:
- Piotr Großmann
---

On macOS, a two-finger scroll on a trackpad or a swipe on a Magic Mouse over the tactical view now pans the map on both axes, in the direction the system's Natural scrolling setting gives. The same scroll over the sidebar still scrolls its strips, and a notched mouse wheel keeps running Sidebar Up and Sidebar Down wherever the pointer is.
